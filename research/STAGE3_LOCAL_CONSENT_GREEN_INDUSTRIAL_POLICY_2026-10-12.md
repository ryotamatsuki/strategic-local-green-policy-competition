Exact primitive conditions and a surviving full-game-only theorem MUST be demonstrated before calling it research novelty.

2. Local participation can be satisfied by symmetric benchmark, so the coalition problem is feasible by construction (status-quo NE weakly qualifies); positive gains are not guaranteed for all primitives.
3. Lump-sum transfers would trivially undo a host-only welfare veto when aggregate gains are positive. Therefore the proposal's **no-transfer** restriction is substantive and requires an institutional rationale, not a universal impossibility claim.
4. The no-conventional-investment model `1/kx →0` also shows numerical asymmetric concentration and local hosting losses under some primitives; **do not claim the mechanism necessarily requires conventional private investment**.
5. The model has fixed plant *locations*. A monopoly continuation means the incumbent's output drops to zero, **not relocation or agglomeration of a mass of mobile firms**. Terminology must be `policy-induced regional exit / production concentration`, not physical relocation.
6. No global market equilibrium uniqueness beyond the existing displayed SPNE; the outside option's selection is explicit.
7. Do not interpret delegated local `d(E_i-Ebar)^2` as the global marginal climate damage of emissions; the Pareto constraint concerns the modeled regional objective.
