## 1. F1 failed its central novelty claim under direct general theory

F1 originally proposed deciding place-based industrial grant clawback/continuation using host-local measurable jobs versus cross-regional net benefits, and adding auditable other-region indicators. The economically interesting *application* remains, but the supposed general new result (misaligned metric yields distorted contract; additional informative signals may help; strategic rival outputs matter) is already derived from older principal-agent and competition theory:

- **Bengt Holmström (1979), 'Moral Hazard and Observability', Bell Journal of Economics 10:74–91**: necessary/sufficient characterization of when extra observed signals improve optimal incentive contracts. https://ideas.repec.org/a/rje/bellje/v10y1979ispringp74-91.html . Abstract-level theorem description; full JSTOR not examined.
- **Holmström & Milgrom (1991), 'Multitask Principal–Agent Analyses: Incentive Contracts, Asset Ownership, and Job Design'**, *Journal of Law, Economics, & Organization*. https://web.stanford.edu/~milgrom/publishedarticles/Multitask%20Principal%20Agent.pdf . Author-hosted complete paper available, intro/read excerpt; incentives geared to partial observables may distort other productive tasks.
- **George Baker (1992), 'Incentive Contracts and Performance Measurement', Journal of Political Economy 100:598–614**, DOI `10.1086/261831`. Publisher abstract explicitly proves misaligned measurable outcomes fail first-best incentives even with risk-neutral agent and studies metric efficiency. https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/261831 .
- **Aggarwal & Samwick (1999)**, *Journal of Finance* 54:1999–2043, DOI `10.1111/0022-1082.00180`: optimal incentives under strategic product competition can weight **own and rivals' outcomes** positively. Even signs/role of other-region outcomes are not automatically new. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/0022-1082.00180 .
- **Wu, Ieong & Thomas (2025)**, *Journal of Corporate Finance* 94, DOI `10.1016/j.jcorpfin.2025.102827`: input-output production-network exposure already enters **relative performance measure design**. Journal abstract only. https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/corfin/v94y2025ics0929119925000951.html .
- **Mazzucato & Rodrik (2026)**, *Industrial and Corporate Change*, DOI `10.1093/icc/dtaf063`, separate measured **additionality** from aggregate **public value** in their industrial-contract taxonomic framework (FULL publisher §2).
- **Fajgelbaum & Gaubert (2025)**, NBER w33517, and **Slattery (2025)**, JPE `10.1086/735509`, characterize spatial incidence and subsidies to firms; evaluating regional net welfare, not gross own-region jobs, is established economics.

**N3 assessment**: A new **industrial policy application/empirical design** that collects otherwise unavailable regional effects may still be valuable; however, under F1's current words there is no new general economic theorem beyond standard metric-informativeness, multitasking, product-market externality and geographic welfare accounting. Merely requiring a national statistic, inserting an extra displaced-job measure or introducing cross-industry network link is **KILLED** as a full-paper theory proposition. Exact joint model is not claimed algebraically identical to a particular precursor, but **no irreducible economic conclusion is specified**. **F1 = NO-GO AS THEORETICAL PROJECT; DO NOT PROCEED STAGE 3.**
