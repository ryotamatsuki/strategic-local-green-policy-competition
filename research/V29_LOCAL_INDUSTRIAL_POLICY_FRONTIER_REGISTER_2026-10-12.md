## C. v2.9 research opportunity decisions

**T1 (theory question; NOT yet a model GO)** — Under what **minimal additional incentive** does strategic nomination of complementary public projects by multiple regional governments change a known centralization–decentralization or welfare/implementability theorem, holding all other economic assumptions constant? Outcome must be a falsifiable strict reversal or impossible/possible mechanism claim, not merely "complements matter." Strongest killer: Armstrong–Vickers 2010 delegated discovery, Schmidbauer 2019 budget competition, Lülfesmann et al. 2015 noncontractible preparation, Cheikbossian 2016 public complements, Cohen et al. 2023 interdependent-project mechanisms. Maturity = LITERATURE-SCREENED / UNRESOLVED. N4 exact-predecessor theorem comparison BEFORE any Stage 3. Priority: one bounded search, not presumptive full-paper.

Maturity = HYPOTHESIS / UNRESOLVED / NOT AUTHORIZED. Kill if project-plan, losing bids, separate grant and borrowing fiscal costs, outcomes and quasi-exogenous assignment cannot be identified. Must enter empirical workflow if pursued.
