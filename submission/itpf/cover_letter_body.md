Please consider the manuscript “Strategic Local Green Policy Competition: Product-Market Rivalry and Instrument Switching” for publication in *International Tax and Public Finance*.

The paper studies interjurisdictional policy competition when local governments simultaneously use firm-specific green-investment support and productive public infrastructure, firms choose both conventional cost-reducing and green investment, and firms subsequently compete in a differentiated product market. The central object is a cross-instrument best response: how one jurisdiction’s infrastructure policy responds to a rival jurisdiction’s green-investment subsidy.

The main result is that product-market substitutability can reverse the sign of this infrastructure response at a unique threshold. The result is established on a certified nonempty global-SPNE region: the analysis explicitly checks nonnegative duopoly, limit-pricing-kink, and monopoly continuations rather than relying only on an interior stationary branch. At the canonical remaining primitives, a matched benchmark without conventional competitive investment does not display the reversal, although the paper does not claim that conventional investment is universally necessary for switching.

The contribution is intentionally narrower than the ingredients of the model. Multi-instrument fiscal competition, productive public inputs, environmental federalism, and simultaneous conventional and green investment are established in the literature. The paper’s result-level distinction is the product-substitutability-driven sign reversal in the same cross-instrument interjurisdictional best response, together with the global-equilibrium qualification and matched benchmark. This positioning is particularly close to the fiscal-competition and public-input literature represented by Hauptmeier, Mittermaier, and Rincke and to the recent infrastructure/fiscal-competition analysis of Morita and Okoshi.

The manuscript is theoretical and uses no empirical dataset. The public project repository contains symbolic and numerical verification materials for the reported analytical and computational checks. The manuscript also contains a transparent generative-AI-use statement consistent with the author’s responsibility for all mathematical results, citations, and substantive conclusions.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
