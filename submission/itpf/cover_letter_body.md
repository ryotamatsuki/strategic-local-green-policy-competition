Please consider the manuscript “Green Subsidies, Public Infrastructure, and Product-Market Rivalry: When Cross-Instrument Responses Reverse” for publication in *International Tax and Public Finance*.

The paper studies interjurisdictional policy competition when local governments simultaneously use firm-specific green-investment support and productive public infrastructure, firms choose both conventional cost-reducing and green investment, and firms subsequently compete in a differentiated product market. The central object is a cross-instrument best response: how one jurisdiction’s infrastructure policy responds to a rival jurisdiction’s green-investment subsidy.

The main result identifies sufficient conditions for a unique product-substitutability threshold at which the infrastructure response to a rival's green subsidy changes from negative to positive. We show that the canonical equilibrium remains globally optimal against policy deviations into duopoly, limit-pricing and monopoly continuations, and that this property extends to a nonempty neighborhood of parameters. A matched benchmark without conventional investment does not display the reversal at the same remaining canonical parameters.

The paper contributes to the literature on fiscal competition and productive public inputs by explaining when product-market rivalry changes the direction of a cross-instrument response. In contrast to established analyses of tax/public-input reaction functions and fiscal competition for firm location, we follow the response to the same rival green-investment subsidy as product substitutability varies, with both conventional and green firm investment endogenous. The matched benchmark identifies how the conventional investment margin can alter the pattern without being necessary in every possible environment.

The manuscript is theoretical and uses no empirical dataset. The public project repository contains symbolic and numerical verification materials for the reported analytical and computational checks. The manuscript also includes an explicit statement describing the use of generative AI tools and the author's responsibility for the submitted work.
