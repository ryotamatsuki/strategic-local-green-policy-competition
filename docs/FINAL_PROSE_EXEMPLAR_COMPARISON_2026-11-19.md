## Priority boundary

Morita and Okoshi's section 4.3 examines **product similarity** and infrastructure, so claiming a first-ever link between substitutability and infrastructure is untenable. Levaggi and Panteghini establish heterogeneous *signs* of fiscal reactions; these signs themselves are not novel. The defensible more specific distinction is the local infrastructure partial best-response to a rival green-investment subsidy after **own subsidy reoptimization**, varying product substitutability with endogenous conventional and green investments, on a genuinely certified nonnegative-game neighborhood. Previous novelty gate classified the contribution DISTINCT BUT NARROW.

Two genuine full-text comparators were covered here; this is not proof the historical Stage10A blueprint and all its required exemplar sources were completely available. Ratings are for the *original* PDF; the revised paper still requires independent whole-PDF editorial retest. No source prose was copied.
