## Exactly what changed

For the initial disclosure edit, only `sections/declarations.tex` was changed. A terse, generic claim and the work-in-progress sentence "Final author-level verification and approval ... required before submission" were replaced by a publication-facing disclosure covering:

1. **Tool category:** OpenAI ChatGPT and AI-assisted coding tools.
2. **Research use:** exploration of model formulation, identifying and organizing related research, symbolic and numerical code assistance, internal critical review.
3. **Writing use:** generating drafts and substantial manuscript restructuring; this is beyond copyediting and requires disclosure.
4. **Validation:** explicit symbolic derivations, exact-rational Python/SymPy comparisons across firm continuation and nonnegative government policy regimes.
5. **Formal-method qualification:** prior selected components had Lean checks; the newly featured two-point global-equilibrium statement **has not itself been formalized as a new Lean theorem**.
6. **Accountability:** AI suggestions are not independent proofs, credible citations by themselves or external peer-review reports; the corresponding author remains responsible for final claims, source accuracy and interpretations.

The revised paragraph is fully within the manuscript, not in an internal audit or cover letter only. No title-page AI footnote was added because one is not required in the official journal-specific instructions.
