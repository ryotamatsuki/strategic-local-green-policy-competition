## Whole-paper coherence and conflict-resolution audit

1. **Single question:** every main section traces to the signed cross-best-response `∂h_A^BR/∂s_B`. The paper is not a general climate-policy welfare treatise.
2. **Terminology/voice:** use "cross-instrument response sign reversal" (not discrete instrument switching) and "canonical global-SPNE bridge" with quantified scope; label empirical predictions hypothetical.
3. **Model vs proof:** no imported tax-base equalization, MNE location choice or capital-mobility channels; the existing staged game and nonnegative strategies are intact.
4. **Theorem/proof order:** clear result statement and plain-language intuition in-body; proof-critical quartic–Bernstein sign bridge and global-policy deviation status visible; routine algebra and branch enumeration in Appendix, including limits and edge cases.
5. **Exhibits:** Figure1 must visualize exactly the theorem and the matched restricted benchmark. No figure may imply `x` is necessary in all parameter vectors.
6. **Limits location:** brief qualifying sentence where each claim arises (abstract/intro/result) plus a *non-duplicative* limitations synthesis in Discussion. A single consolidated section is not mandatory; no material assumption is buried until the end.
7. **Conflict resolved — concise Matsumoto vs rich Lai:** concise theorem main text, then one carefully decomposed mechanism paragraph with objective terms, not an overly compressed ambiguous result or a six-page contextual essay.
8. **Conflict resolved — M&O close subject vs our distinct object:** use M&O as policy-regime writing example and confront it directly in literature; do **not** imitate its beneficial-competition claim, location model, or absolute welfare framing.
9. **Conflict resolved — "strong" opener vs conservative novelty:** emphasize an economically meaningful falsifiable conditional prediction while writing the exact scope qualifier next to it; neither inflated novelty nor disclaimer-led opening.
10. **Conflict resolved — elegance vs globality:** no proof-shortening move may bypass or change the kink/monopoly global-deviation check. If it cannot be made reader-friendly without omission, preserve an explicit bridge and send technical steps to appendix.
