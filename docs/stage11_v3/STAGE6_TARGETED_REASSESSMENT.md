## B02 — targeted economic diagnostic, *not* an unconditional theorem

Fix all primitives other than \`θ,d\` at canonical values: \`k_x=4, k_g=18, μ=9/10, ν=6/5, κ=4/5, e=11/10, β=17/10, ξ=1/10, m=2,\bar E=0\`. The rival enters the continuation through \`w_B=m+(μ/k_g)s_B+νh_B\`. For *equal changes* in \`y_B=(μ/k_g)s_B+νh_B\`, the local best-response derivative is the same whether the change is initiated by the rival subsidy or infrastructure, on any differentiable branch with a valid fixed rival competitive index; on kink/non-unique points compare best-response **sets**, not derivatives. The two instruments are a shared shock **index**, not separately identified causal treatments.

From the quadratic regional objective \`W(s,h,w_B)\` on the strict interior active-duopoly branch, let
\`N=W_{sh}W_{sw_B}-W_{ss}W_{hw_B}\`. The environmental-target component of the Hessian is \`-d E_z E_z^T\` (rank one, as emissions are affine in policy on the branch). Thus the \`d^2\` terms cancel in \`N=N_0(θ)+d N_1(θ)\`.

**Reproducible exact-rational boundary for the CANONICAL remaining primitives (not all primitives):**

Set \`u=θ²\` and
\`A(u)=50u⁴−1000u³+7073u²−20525u+20604\`,
\`B(u)=242500u⁴−901550u³−11337491u²+73636958u−116485656\`.

Then
\`d*(θ)=−14400 A(θ²)/B(θ²)\` for \`0≤θ≤1\`, using the continuous limit at 0; it is the unique zero of the **interior profiled cross-gradient numerator**, not a global best-response switching locus for unrestricted policies.

An independent Hessian construction using only model primitives and exact SymPy rationals gives:
- \`N_1(θ)/θ > 0\` for \`0<θ≤1\` (positive factor times \`(θ−2)(θ+2)B(θ²)\` divided by a positive squared denominator).
- \`d*'(θ)/θ = −950400 C(θ²)/B(θ²)²<0\` for \`0<θ≤1\`, with \`C(u)=5982500u⁶−138307700u⁵+1323988950u⁴−6895925900u³+21009569023u²−35776083656u+26474309296\`. Certified rational **zero counts on [0,1]**: A=0, B=0, C=0, with A(0)>0, B(0)<0, C(0)>0. The two cubic factors of the profiled denominator have zero roots in θ∈[0,1]. Thus no unreported pole invalidates the interval sign.
- Conditional on strict negative-definite own-policy Hessian, *interior* \`∂h_A^BR/∂y_B\` has the same sign as \`d−d*(θ)\` for positive θ. At θ=0 rival policies have no cross-market effect and the derivative vanishes; \`d*(0+)\` is a limit only, not a reaction zero.

### The economic and feasibility filter

This is not a claim that \`d=1.5\` and \`d=3\` are global policy equilibria. Stage11's independent all-policy attack at \`θ=.9\` finds:
- \`d=1.5\`: interior candidate is feasible but has a more profitable rival-exclusion deviation, welfare advantage about \`+0.29564\` → **NOT global SPNE**.
- \`d=2\`: original rational canonical configuration has global SPNE over \`θ∈[0,1]\`; at θ=.9 the true response is positive, with unique crossing \`θ*=0.7738043861…\`.
- \`d=3\`: symmetric interior FOC at θ=.9 gives \`h≈−0.3256\`, violating nonnegativity → **CANDIDATE INFEASIBLE**.

The certified **nonempty open primitive neighborhood around \`d=2\`** already established in Proposition \`prop:global-spne\`, combined with continuity and the strictly decreasing \`d*(θ)\` crossing near \`θ*=.774\`, guarantees some *unspecified but nondegenerate* local interval of \`d\` and neighboring θ where the global best-response has opposite signs on either side of the boundary. It does **not** certify a numerical width, the entire graph of \`d*(θ)\` as globally feasible, or a global classification for the extremes above. A numerical grid is not proof of a globally feasible band.

### Candidate new one-sentence contribution

> Within a locally globally certified fixed-location green-policy game, the sign of infrastructure's response to a common rival competitiveness shock can be diagnosed by a **joint rivalry–territorial-target boundary** after the local government reoptimizes its own subsidy; the boundary is monotone in product substitutability on the interior branch, but many interior sign configurations fail the global policy-equilibrium test.

**Falsification design, not observed evidence:** Conditional on exogenously comparable shocks to the same rival \`y_B\`, comparable product-market exposure, independently chosen local policies and a correctly measured marginal target-discipline parameter, a local sign shift across a certified feasible segment of the boundary should be observed. Failure to observe it in that segment, or systematically different local responses to shock bundles producing the same \`y_B\` within one continuation, would contradict the model. General-equilibrium simultaneous policy readjustment, a change of active branch, and a level difference do not isolate this derivative.

**Economic-value caveat:** this boundary follows an affine-in-\`d\` IFT numerator in one canonical LQ specification, and \`d\` is a latent policy-objective weight, not an observed emissions target stringency. Existing public-input reaction and sign-switching theories preempt any broad novelty claim. Whether an ITPF editor views this certified narrow diagnostic as an important enough independent contribution **remains unresolved**. Hence the Stage6 originality gate is **not unconditional GO**, and the original Stage11 desk-reject critique is not overturned.
