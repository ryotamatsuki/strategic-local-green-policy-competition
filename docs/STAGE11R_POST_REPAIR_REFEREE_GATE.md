## 2. Referee A — novelty and mechanism

### Attack A1 — “Hauptmeier et al. already have instrument switching.”

- Severity: `MAJOR BUT FIXABLE`.
- Evidence: Hauptmeier, Mittermaier, and Rincke (2012) model simultaneous business-tax and productive-public-input competition and report cross-instrument reactions; in particular, a neighbor tax cut can induce an own-tax adjustment together with higher public-input provision.
- Current response: the manuscript no longer claims novelty for cross-instrument reactions or for the qualitative use of infrastructure as a response to a rival fiscal policy.
- Required fix: state the Hauptmeier overlap more explicitly and define novelty only as the unique product-substitutability threshold that moves the same cross-instrument derivative through zero on a certified global-SPNE region.
- Theory reopened: `NO`.
- Resolved: `YES` in `sections/related_literature.tex`.

### Attack A2 — “Morita–Okoshi already have subsidy + infrastructure + Cournot + a threshold.”

- Severity: `MAJOR BUT FIXABLE`.
- Evidence: Morita and Okoshi (2025) combine fiscal competition, infrastructure, location, Cournot competition, and threshold effects.
- Current response: the paper distinguishes timing and object: Morita–Okoshi study infrastructure/fiscal competition around MNE location and transport costs, whereas the present theorem is a simultaneous local cross-policy best-response sign reversal indexed by product substitutability after endogenous firm investment.
- Required fix: keep the distinction theorem-level and avoid ingredient-count novelty.
- Theory reopened: `NO`.
- Resolved: `YES`.

### Attack A3 — “Hauptmeier + Strandholm is the whole paper.”

- Severity: `MAJOR BUT FIXABLE`.
- Evidence: multi-instrument local reactions are known; simultaneous conventional and green R&D is known from Strandholm, Espinola-Arredondo, and Muñoz-García (2025).
- Current response: the paper claims neither component. The surviving object is the rivalry-indexed sign change in the government policy network plus a matched same-primitives no-`x` separation.
- Required fix: keep the contribution narrow and do not revive “dual-investment necessity.”
- Theory reopened: `NO`.
- Resolved: `YES`.

### Novelty verdict

No exact prior-art absorption was identified in the post-repair re-search. The result is potentially novel at theorem level, but narrow. The paper should not be positioned as a generic theory of instrument switching or as the first multi-instrument green-policy competition model.
