\section{Robustness and Scope}
Mandatory robustness checks are: (i) differentiated Bertrand competition with an open interior parameter set preserving the sign reversal; and (ii) partial local capture of firm surplus, $\omega<1$. The following are outside the frozen baseline and require a theory-change record if introduced: endogenous plant location, multi-plant capital allocation, asymmetric foreign governments, green public procurement, financing constraints, dynamics or learning, heterogeneous firms, and hard public budget constraints.
