\section{Welfare}
The coordinated benchmark maximizes
\[
W_A+W_B
\]
over $(s_A,h_A,s_B,h_B)$. Decentralized governments do not fully internalize cross-jurisdictional consumer-surplus, producer-surplus/business-stealing, and emissions effects. Hence decentralized and coordinated policy portfolios generally differ. This welfare comparison is a supporting result rather than the headline novelty claim.
