\section{Model}
There are two jurisdictions, $i\in\{A,B\}$, each hosting one incumbent firm. Plant locations are fixed. The game has complete information and three stages. In Stage 1, local governments simultaneously choose a firm-specific green-investment subsidy $s_i$ and shared green public infrastructure $h_i$. In Stage 2, firms simultaneously choose conventional competitive investment $x_i$ and green investment $g_i$. In Stage 3, firms compete in a differentiated Cournot product market.

A representative consumer has gross utility
\[
V(q_A,q_B)=a(q_A+q_B)-\frac12(q_A^2+q_B^2)-\theta q_Aq_B,
\]
so inverse demand is
\[
p_i=a-q_i-\theta q_j,\qquad 0\leq\theta\leq1.
\]

Firm $i$ has marginal cost
\[
c_i=c-x_i-\mu g_i-\nu h_i,
\]
and investment cost
\[
C_i(x_i,g_i)=\frac{k_x}{2}x_i^2+\frac{k_g}{2}g_i^2.
\]
Its profit is
\[
\pi_i=(p_i-c_i)q_i-\frac{k_x}{2}x_i^2-\frac{k_g}{2}g_i^2+s_i g_i.
\]
Territorial emissions are
\[
E_i=e q_i-\beta g_i-\xi h_i.
\]

Consumer surplus is
\[
CS=\frac12(q_A^2+q_B^2)+\theta q_Aq_B.
\]
Local government $i$ maximizes
\[
W_i=\frac12CS+PS_i-\frac{\kappa}{2}h_i^2-\frac d2(E_i-\bar E_i)^2,
\]
where
\[
PS_i=q_i^2-\frac{k_x}{2}x_i^2-\frac{k_g}{2}g_i^2.
\]
The subsidy payment $s_ig_i$ is treated as an intraregional transfer and cancels from regional welfare.
